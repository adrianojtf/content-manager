# Product Vision

## Vision

Create a centralized platform that allows a creator to publish video content across multiple social platforms and transform audience interactions into structured intelligence and actionable content decisions.

## Differentiator

The product should not stop at:

> "What happened to my video?"

It should evolve toward:

> "What is my audience telling me, how should I respond, and what should I do with the next piece of content?"

## Product Loop

`Content → Publication → Audience → Evaluation → Decision → Action → Learning`

## Initial User

A single creator using the platform privately for validation.

## Evolution

```text
Private MVP
    ↓
Creator Intelligence Tool
    ↓
Multi-account Creator Platform
    ↓
Professional / Agency Platform
    ↓
SaaS
```

## AI / JEV Position

JEV is a bounded intelligence capability.

It evaluates normalized context and produces structured analysis, evaluation and recommendations.

JEV does not own:

- persistence;
- authorization;
- social API access;
- domain state transitions;
- publication;
- deterministic business rules.

## Product Principles

1. Platform integrations are replaceable.
2. Domain rules are platform-independent.
3. JEV is accessed through an application port.
4. AI output is structured and auditable.
5. AI recommendations do not automatically become actions.
6. Historical audience intelligence belongs to the product.
7. The system learns from comments and content performance over time.
